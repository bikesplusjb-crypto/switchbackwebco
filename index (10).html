§ 02 — Why

Why hire a human instead of a website builder.

01

You own everything.

Your domain stays in your name. Your site can move anywhere. No platform lock-in, no held-hostage situations, no surprise renewal hikes. The site is your asset, not someone else's subscription.

02

Built fast.

Most one-page sites are ready in days, bigger sites in one to three weeks. You're not going to wait a month for a first draft. We agree on direction, we build it, you approve it, it goes live.

03

No monthly tax.

Pay once for the build. After that, pay only when you want a change — or choose a care plan if you'd rather it was handled. Nothing is required, and a care plan can be cancelled any month.

04

Local Florida, real human.

We're on the Treasure Coast. Every message gets a real reply from the person who built your site, usually the same day. No support tickets, no chatbots, no "your request has been received."
